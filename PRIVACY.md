# Privacy policy

AMC (Agent Mail Client) is open-source software that each person runs on their own server (the Host).

- **Where your data goes:** AMC reads, labels and drafts mail in the Google and IMAP accounts you connect. That mail and the OAuth tokens stay on your own Host. Nothing is sent to the AMC authors or any third party.
- **Google user data:** AMC uses the Gmail API only to show, organise and draft your own mail in your own AMC instance. It does not transfer Google user data to others, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. AMC's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
- **Agents:** AI agents you choose to run (for example, your own Claude Code or Codex) may read your mail on your Host under your own accounts with those providers.
- **Deleting data:** to delete your data, remove it from your Host, and revoke AMC's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/ralfboltshauser/agent-mail-client/issues.
