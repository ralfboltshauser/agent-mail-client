Brand guide

Agent Mail Client is keyboard-first mail you can work through with an agent. Coarse Stencil is the primary mark.

Logo and app icon

Horizontal lockup

Use the symbol with the outlined AMC wordmark for project introductions and release material. Keep at least one quarter of the mark height clear around the lockup. Use the standard lockup at 100 px wide or larger.

Symbol on its own

Use the symbol for compact placements. Keep the three shapes upright and their seams open. Use the paper mark on dark backgrounds and the ink mark on light backgrounds.

Menu bar All Accounts

The Dock uses the black mark on a white tile. The menu bar and All Accounts mark take their color from their surroundings.
