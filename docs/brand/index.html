Brand guide

Agent Mail Client is keyboard-first mail you can work through with an agent. The Coarse Stencil mark turns the folds of a letter into a bold pixel M. Wide seams keep the three pieces legible on small surfaces.

Logo and app icon

Horizontal lockup

Use the symbol with the outlined AMC wordmark for project introductions and release material. Keep at least one quarter of the mark height clear around the lockup. Use the standard lockup at 100 px wide or larger.

Symbol on its own

Use the symbol for compact placements. Keep the three shapes upright and their seams open. Use the paper mark on dark backgrounds and the ink mark on light backgrounds.

Menu bar All Accounts

The Dock uses a paper mark on an ink tile. The menu bar and All Accounts mark take their color from their surroundings.

Name and voice

Use Agent Mail Client (AMC) on first mention in public copy and AMC in compact labels. Write amc for the executable and agent-mail-client for the repository slug.

Say what happened and what the person can do next. “Draft ready to review” makes the handoff explicit. “Sync failed. Retry” gives the next action.
